- A **primary key** (PK) identifies a row. A **foreign key** points at another table's primary key.
- `bookings` links members to facilities: a many-to-many relationship stored as two one-to-many ones.
- `recommendedby` points back into `members`, so resolving it means reading `members` twice under two aliases.
- There are two Darren Smiths (1 and 37). Names collide; keys don't.
