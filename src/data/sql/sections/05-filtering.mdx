`'Tennis'` is a string. `"Tennis"` is a column called Tennis. `IN (…)` matches exact values from a list; `LIKE` matches a pattern.

<LikePlayground client:visible />

- `~` is a regex match in Postgres, `~*` case-insensitive.
- AND binds tighter than OR: `a or b and c` is `a or (b and c)`. Parenthesise anything that mixes them.
