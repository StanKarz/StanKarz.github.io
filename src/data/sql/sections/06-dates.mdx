A bare date compared with a timestamp means midnight on that date.

<BookingTimeline client:visible />

`date_trunc` keeps the larger units, so grouping by `date_trunc('month', …)` keeps September 2012 and September 2013 apart. `extract(month …)` doesn't.

```sql
-- slots booked per month
select date_trunc('month', starttime) as month, sum(slots)
from cd.bookings
group by 1
order by 1;
```
